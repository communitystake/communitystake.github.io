About

Our purpose

A Sint Maarten foundation that provides financing for local real estate projects built with local businesses.

Community Stake Foundation (CSF) is a Sint Maarten private foundation (stichting particulier fonds). It was founded on 8 January 2026 and is registered with the Sint Maarten Chamber of Commerce under no. 32712.

Sint Maarten’s real estate market is largely owned by people and companies based off the island. Local residents often see the island change around them without having a real share in it.

CSF was set up to help change that. We provide financing for real estate projects on Sint Maarten, mainly residential housing. We don’t undertake the projects ourselves. Developers do.

As a condition of our financing agreements, developers are required to use local Sint Maarten businesses and service providers for construction, real estate services and, later, maintenance and repair. By local we mean long-term residents of Sint Maarten, particularly those who have not had the benefit of established business networks, and the businesses they own.

Just as important, we want local people involved in the financial side of each project. Working with banks, notaries and lawyers on real transactions builds the experience and confidence to undertake projects on their own.

What guides us

Local first

Developers of the projects we finance are required to use Sint Maarten businesses owned by long-term residents.

Long-term thinking

We finance projects that serve the island for years, not quick turnover.

Shared know-how

We want long-term residents to become comfortable with the financial side of development, so they can lead projects of their own.

Careful stewardship

The foundation is governed under its articles and Sint Maarten law, with a Board that manages its affairs.
