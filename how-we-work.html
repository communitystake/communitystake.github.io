How We Work

How we work

We provide the financing. Developers undertake the projects, using local businesses and involving local people in the financial side.

  1. Find projects to finance.

    We look for local real estate projects, mainly residential housing, that make sense for the island and can be delivered by local teams.

  2. Agree the financing.

    CSF provides financing to the developer under a written financing agreement. Terms are decided case by case.

  3. Use local businesses.

    As a condition of the financing, the developer uses Sint Maarten businesses and service providers for construction, real estate services and, later, maintenance and repair. Local means long-term residents of Sint Maarten, particularly those who have not had the benefit of established business networks. Our local vendor list helps developers find them.

  4. Build financial know-how.

    We want local people involved in the financial side of each project, working with banks, notaries and lawyers on the transactions. The goal is for them to gain the experience and confidence to undertake projects on their own.

  5. Reinvest the returns.

    Returns on our financing go back into financing future local projects.
