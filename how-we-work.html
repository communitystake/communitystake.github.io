How We Work

How we work

From finding the right projects to reinvesting the returns, local businesses are part of every step.

  1. Find the right projects.

    We look for local real estate projects, mainly residential housing, that make sense for the island and can be delivered by local teams.

  2. Finance and partner.

    CSF provides financing to local developers and builders, or partners with them on projects. Each arrangement is decided case by case.

  3. Build with local businesses.

    For construction, real estate services and, later, building maintenance and repair, we aim to work with Sint Maarten contractors and service providers. Our local vendor list helps us find them.

  4. Hold, manage and reinvest.

    Once a property is complete, it may be held for the long term or sold. Net returns go back into future local projects.
