Local Vendors

Local vendor directory

We keep a list of Sint Maarten businesses that developers of the projects we finance can call on.

Developers of CSF-financed projects are required to prioritize local businesses and service providers, and vendors in our directory may be referred to them. By local we mean businesses owned by long-term residents of Sint Maarten, particularly those who have not had the benefit of established business networks.

We are especially interested in:

  • Construction trades: general contracting, masonry, carpentry, electrical, plumbing, roofing, and similar trades
  • Real estate services: agents, surveyors, appraisers, legal, notarial, and similar services
  • Property maintenance and repair: for buildings once they are complete

It takes a few minutes. Tell us about your business and the work you do. We’ll keep your details on file and get in touch if a suitable opportunity comes up.

Being listed does not guarantee work. Referrals are at CSF’s discretion, and developers make their own hiring decisions.

Our forms are provided mainly for efficiency and are our preferred way of collecting your details. However, if you would rather not share this information through a form but still wish to make an inquiry, you can email us directly at communitystakefoundation@gmail.com, and we will do our best to follow up with you privately.
