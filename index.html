Community Stake Foundation · Sint Maarten

A local stake in Sint Maarten property.

Community Stake Foundation is a Sint Maarten foundation dedicated to helping local residents take part in, and benefit from, the island’s real estate market.

Much of Sint Maarten’s property is owned off-island. We want more of the value created here to stay here, with the people and businesses who live and work on the island.

What we do

Local development

We finance and partner on local real estate projects, such as residential housing.

Local businesses

We aim to use Sint Maarten contractors and service providers to build, sell, manage and maintain our properties.

Lasting benefit

Returns from one project go back into the next one.
