Community Stake Foundation · Sint Maarten

A local stake in Sint Maarten real estate and real estate development.

Community Stake Foundation is a Sint Maarten foundation that helps long-term local residents who have historically had limited access to development financing bring their own real estate projects to life through financing and mentorship. Developers undertake the projects. We assist with financing and provide mentorship in working with banks, lawyers, notaries and other financial and regulatory advisers.

Where possible, developers are encouraged to use local businesses owned by long-term residents of Sint Maarten, particularly those without established, generational business networks. Through this mentorship, we aim to build their confidence and independence so they can take on future projects of their own, without Community Stake's involvement.

What we do

Project financing

We provide financing for local real estate projects, such as residential housing and multi-unit commercial buildings. Developers undertake the projects.

Local businesses

Our financing agreements require developers to prioritize locally owned Sint Maarten businesses for construction, real estate services, and later maintenance and repair.

Lasting benefit

Any positive financial returns on our financing go back into future local projects.

Why it matters

What we mean by local

By local we mean long-term residents of Sint Maarten, particularly those who have not had the benefit of established business networks, and the businesses they own.

Building financial know-how

We want local people involved in the financial side of each project, working with banks, notaries and lawyers on real transactions, so they are ready to undertake projects on their own.
