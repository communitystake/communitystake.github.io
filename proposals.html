Project Financing

Apply for project financing

Wishing to undertake a real estate development project on Sint Maarten? If you are a local resident, tell us about it and the financing you may need. We’ll see if we can help.

CSF provides financing for local real estate projects, mainly residential housing and multi-unit commercial developments. You undertake the project, and CSF provides financing. You can apply as an individual, a business or a group, and your project can be at an early stage.

As a condition of our financing, developers are required to prioritize local Sint Maarten businesses and service providers, meaning businesses owned by long-term residents of Sint Maarten, particularly those who have not had the benefit of established business networks. We also want local people involved in the financial side of the project, working with banks, notaries and lawyers.

Submitting a request creates no commitment on either side. If there is a fit, we will follow up.

Our forms are provided mainly for efficiency and are our preferred way of collecting your details. However, if you would rather not share this information through a form but still wish to make an inquiry, you can email us directly at communitystakefoundation@gmail.com, and we will do our best to follow up with you privately.
